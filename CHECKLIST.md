## D. Each design system (×3) — six required sections

| # | Section | Must contain | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Colour roles** | background, surface, text, muted, border, accent **+ contrast ratios printed** | ☐ |
| D2 | **Type roles** | display, heading, body, label, **with sizes and weights, in your own content** | ☐ |
| D3 | **Spacing and shape** | a spacing scale, corners, borders, shadow | ☐ |
| D4 | **Components** (14) | primary button, secondary button, link, text input, select, checkbox, radio, toggle, tabs, filter chips, card with a real item, list rows, badge, modal/detail panel | ☐ |
| D5 | **Control states** (6) | rest, hover, focus, pressed, selected, disabled | ☐ |
| D6 | **UI states** (4) | empty, loading, error, success | ☐ |

**Per system: 6 colour roles + 4 type roles + 14 components + 6 control states + 4 UI states.**
**Across three systems: 18 colour roles, 12 type roles, 42 components, 18 control states, 12 UI states.**
